# What to Mix With Vodka: 15 Easy Mixers & Drink Ideas

The best things to mix with vodka are soda water, tonic water, cranberry juice, orange juice, ginger beer, and fresh citrus like lime and lemon. Because vodka is clean and nearly flavor-neutral, it pairs with almost anything — sweet, sour, bitter, or bubbly. Start with a 1.5 oz pour of vodka over ice, add 4–5 oz of your chosen mixer, and finish with a squeeze of fresh fruit for a balanced, easy drink.

Whether you reach for a crisp, smooth bottle from the [Touch Vodka collection](/products) or a flavored expression, the right mixer turns a simple pour into a drink worth lingering over. This guide breaks down the most reliable vodka mixers, quick recipes you can build in under two minutes, and tips for matching mixers to each Touch expression.

## The Best Mixers for Vodka

Vodka's neutrality is its superpower. Distilled to a clean profile — Touch Vodka is **10× distilled**, **charcoal-filtered**, and built on a **winter wheat** base with **mineral-rich spring water** — it lets your mixer shine rather than fighting it. Here are the categories that never miss.

### Sparkling & Bubbly Mixers

- **Soda water (club soda):** Zero sugar, pure lift. The lightest way to lengthen a drink.
- **Tonic water:** Adds a gentle quinine bitterness that balances vodka's smoothness.
- **Ginger beer:** Spicy, punchy, and the backbone of a Moscow Mule.
- **Lemon-lime soda:** Sweet and fizzy for an easy crowd-pleaser.
- **Sparkling water with fruit:** Flavored seltzers make an instant low-calorie highball.

### Juice Mixers

- **Cranberry juice:** Tart and ruby-red — the classic vodka cranberry.
- **Orange juice:** Brunch in a glass; the foundation of a Screwdriver.
- **Pineapple juice:** Tropical and forgiving, great with citrus vodkas.
- **Grapefruit juice:** Bittersweet and refreshing.
- **Tomato juice:** The savory base of a Bloody Mary.

### Fresh & Simple Add-Ins

- **Fresh lime or lemon:** Brightens any pour instantly.
- **Cucumber slices:** Cooling and clean.
- **Fresh mint:** Garden-fresh aroma.
- **Simple syrup:** Rounds out sour mixers.
- **Bitters:** A dash adds complexity without sweetness.

## Easy Vodka Drinks You Can Build in Two Minutes

You don't need a cocktail shaker or a shelf of liqueurs. These easy vodka drinks come together with ice, a glass, and one or two mixers.

### Vodka Soda With Lime

A clean, low-sugar classic that lets the spirit speak. It's the perfect showcase for the ultra-clean, charcoal-filtered character of [Touch One](/products/touch-one).

**Ingredients**

- 1.5 oz Touch One vodka
- 4 oz soda water
- 1 lime wedge
- Ice

**Method:** Fill a highball glass with ice, pour the vodka, top with soda water, squeeze in the lime, and give it one gentle stir.

### Vodka Cranberry (Cape Codder)

Tart, refreshing, and endlessly customizable. The smooth, classic profile of [Touch Artisan](/products/touch-artisan) keeps it polished.

**Ingredients**

- 1.5 oz Touch Artisan vodka
- 4 oz cranberry juice
- 1 lime wedge
- Ice

**Method:** Build over ice in a rocks or highball glass, stir, and garnish with the lime.

### Tropical Vodka Cooler

Bright citrus meets pineapple for a sunny sipper. Reach for [Touch Key Lime](/products/touch-key-lime) to lean into the tropical citrus notes.

**Ingredients**

- 1.5 oz Touch Key Lime vodka
- 3 oz pineapple juice
- Splash of soda water
- Mint sprig
- Ice

**Method:** Shake the vodka and pineapple juice with ice, strain over fresh ice, top with soda, and slap a mint sprig for aroma.

## How Mixers Affect Calories and Sugar

A common surprise: the **vodka isn't where most of the calories come from** in a mixed drink — the mixer is. At **80 proof (40% ABV)**, a 1.5 oz pour of vodka is roughly 95 calories and contains no carbs or sugar. It's the juice, soda, and syrups that stack up.

### Smart Swaps to Keep It Light

- Swap sugary soda for **soda water** plus a splash of juice.
- Use **fresh citrus** instead of sweetened mixes.
- Choose **diet tonic** or flavored seltzer over regular tonic.
- Skip the simple syrup and let the mixer's natural sweetness carry the drink.

## Tips for Better Vodka Mixed Drinks

Small details separate a forgettable pour from a great one.

- **Use plenty of fresh ice.** Old ice melts fast and waters down your drink.
- **Squeeze fresh citrus.** Bottled juice can't match the brightness of a fresh lime.
- **Chill your vodka.** A cold spirit mixes smoother and tastes cleaner.
- **Mind the ratio.** A 1:3 or 1:4 vodka-to-mixer ratio is a reliable starting point.
- **Garnish with intent.** A wheel, a sprig, or a twist adds aroma before the first sip.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the easiest thing to mix with vodka?

Soda water and fresh lime is the easiest, lowest-effort vodka mix. Just pour 1.5 oz of vodka over ice, top with soda water, and squeeze in a lime. It's refreshing, low in sugar, and showcases a clean vodka like Touch One beautifully.

### What mixes well with flavored vodka?

Flavored vodka pairs best with mixers that complement its profile. Touch Key Lime loves pineapple juice and soda, Touch Ruby works with lemon-lime soda or sparkling water, and Touch Orange shines with orange juice or ginger beer. Lighter mixers let the flavor come through without competing.

### What can I mix with vodka without juice?

For juice-free options, try soda water, tonic water, ginger beer, or flavored seltzer, finished with fresh citrus, cucumber, or mint. These keep the drink crisp and low in sugar while still feeling complete.

### Does the mixer affect how strong the drink is?

The mixer doesn't change the alcohol content of the vodka itself — a 1.5 oz pour is the same strength regardless of what you add. A larger mixer pour simply dilutes the drink and makes it taste lighter, while less mixer keeps it spirit-forward.
