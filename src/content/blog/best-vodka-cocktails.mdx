# Best Vodka Cocktails: 10 Recipes Every Bar Should Know

The best vodka cocktails include the Moscow Mule, Cosmopolitan, Espresso Martini, Bloody Mary, and the classic Vodka Martini. Because vodka is clean and nearly flavor-neutral, it's the most versatile base spirit in any bar — it lets citrus, coffee, berries, and bitters take center stage. ## The Timeless Classics

These are the vodka cocktails that defined the category. Master them first and you can build almost anything.

### Moscow Mule

Crisp, spicy, and served in a frosty copper mug. The ultra-clean profile of [Touch One](/products/touch-one) keeps the ginger and lime sharp.

**Ingredients**

- 2 oz Touch One vodka
- 4 oz ginger beer
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- Lime wedge and ice

**Method:** Fill a copper mug or highball with ice. Add vodka and lime juice, top with ginger beer, stir gently, and garnish with a lime wedge.

### Vodka Martini

Elegant and spirit-forward — the ultimate test of a smooth vodka. Use [Touch Artisan](/products/touch-artisan) for its smooth premium character.

**Ingredients**

- 2.5 oz Touch Artisan vodka
- 0.5 oz dry vermouth
- Lemon twist or olives
- Ice

**Method:** Stir the vodka and vermouth over ice for 30 seconds until well chilled. Strain into a chilled coupe and garnish with a lemon twist or olives.

### Cosmopolitan

Tart, ruby-pink, and effortlessly stylish.

**Ingredients**

- 1.5 oz vodka
- 1 oz cranberry juice
- 0.5 oz triple sec
- 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
- Ice

**Method:** Shake all ingredients with ice until cold, then strain into a chilled martini glass. Garnish with a lime wheel.

### Bloody Mary

The savory brunch icon, endlessly customizable.

**Ingredients**

- 1.5 oz vodka
- 3 oz tomato juice
- 0.5 oz lemon juice
- 2 dashes hot sauce
- 2 dashes Worcestershire sauce
- Celery, salt, pepper

**Method:** Roll (gently pour back and forth) the ingredients over ice to mix without over-diluting, then garnish with celery, a lemon wedge, and a pinch of salt and pepper.

## Modern Favorites

These newer classics have earned a permanent spot on cocktail menus.

### Espresso Martini

Rich, bittersweet, and wide-awake. A smooth vodka keeps the coffee from turning harsh.

**Ingredients**

- 2 oz vodka
- 1 oz fresh espresso (cooled)
- 0.5 oz coffee liqueur
- 0.5 oz simple syrup
- Ice

**Method:** Shake hard with ice for 15 seconds to build that signature foam, then double-strain into a chilled coupe and garnish with three coffee beans.

### Vodka Soda Spritz

The low-sugar, all-day refresher. Try [Touch Orange](/products/touch-orange) for a bold citrus lift.

**Ingredients**

- 1.5 oz Touch Orange vodka
- 4 oz soda water
- Orange slice
- Ice

**Method:** Build over ice in a highball, top with soda water, stir once, and garnish with an orange slice.

## Tips for Better Vodka Cocktails

- **Start with a smooth spirit.** A clean, charcoal-filtered vodka means a cleaner cocktail. Understanding [how vodka is made](/blog/how-is-vodka-made) explains why distillation matters.
- **Use fresh citrus.** Bottled juice can't compete with a fresh-squeezed lime or lemon.
- **Chill your glassware.** A cold glass keeps spirit-forward drinks crisp.
- **Shake with citrus, stir without.** Shake drinks with juice to aerate; stir clear, spirit-only drinks to keep them silky.
- **Mind your mixers.** The vodka is mostly calorie-light — it's the mixer that adds sugar. See [what to mix with vodka](/blog/what-to-mix-with-vodka) for lighter options.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most popular vodka cocktail?

The Moscow Mule and the Cosmopolitan are among the most popular vodka cocktails, alongside the Espresso Martini and Bloody Mary. The Moscow Mule's mix of vodka, ginger beer, and lime makes it a year-round favorite that's easy to build at home.

### What is the easiest vodka cocktail to make?

A Vodka Soda Spritz is the easiest — just pour 1.5 oz of vodka over ice, top with soda water, and add a citrus slice. It takes under a minute, uses one mixer, and showcases a clean vodka like Touch One or Touch Orange.

### What vodka is best for cocktails?

A smooth, neutral vodka works best because it lets the other ingredients shine. Touch Vodka is 10× distilled and charcoal-filtered at 80 proof (40% ABV), giving cocktails a clean base. For spirit-forward drinks like martinis, the smoothness matters even more.

### Should vodka cocktails be shaken or stirred?

Shake vodka cocktails that contain citrus or juice — like a Cosmopolitan or Espresso Martini — to chill and aerate them. Stir clear, spirit-only drinks like a Vodka Martini to keep them silky and crystal-clear.
